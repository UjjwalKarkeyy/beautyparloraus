Henna Tattoo

Celebrate tradition and beauty with our intricate henna tattoo artistry. Using only 100% natural henna paste, our artists craft stunning designs — from delicate florals to elaborate bridal patterns — that stain the skin a rich, warm brown lasting 1–3 weeks.

Perfect for weddings, festivals, and special occasions, every design is drawn freehand by our skilled henna artists. We offer custom design consultations and can cater to any style from minimalist modern to traditional South Asian or Arabic patterns.

  • Traditional henna art
  • Custom designs
  • Bridal henna packages
  • Natural & organic henna
  • Lasts 1–3 weeks
  • Safe for most skin types
