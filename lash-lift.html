Lash Lift / Perm

Transform your natural lashes with a semi-permanent lift and curl that opens up the eyes beautifully, making your lashes appear longer and fuller — all without a single extension. The treatment uses a safe perming solution to curl each lash from the root.

Results last 6–8 weeks, making this the perfect low-maintenance treatment for those who want gorgeous lashes without the daily mascara routine. Add a lash tint for even more impact.

  • Lash lift & curl
  • Lash perm
  • Keratin lash treatment
  • Lash tint add-on
  • Lasts 6–8 weeks
  • No extensions needed
