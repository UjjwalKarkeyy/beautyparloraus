Threading

Our expert threading technique uses a fine cotton thread twisted and rolled across the skin to precisely remove unwanted hair from the root. This ancient art form delivers perfectly defined brows and facial hair removal with minimal irritation — ideal for all skin types, including sensitive skin.

Unlike waxing, threading doesn't involve any chemicals or heat, making it the safest option for those on certain skincare medications or with reactive skin.

  • Brow shaping & defining
  • Upper lip threading
  • Chin threading
  • Full face threading
  • Forehead & sides
  • Suitable for sensitive skin
