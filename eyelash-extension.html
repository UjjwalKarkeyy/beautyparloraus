Eyelash Extension

Wake up every morning looking effortlessly beautiful. Our expert lash artists individually apply premium-quality synthetic extensions to each of your natural lashes, creating a fully customised look tailored to your eye shape, lifestyle and aesthetic preference.

From a subtle natural enhancement to a glamorous full-volume set, we have a style to suit every client. Lash extensions last 4–6 weeks with proper care and can be maintained with infills.

  • Classic extensions
  • Hybrid extensions
  • Volume & mega volume
  • Wet look & ombre
  • Bottom lash extensions
  • Lash infills available
