Brow Henna

Brow henna is a plant-based colouring treatment that stains both the skin and the brow hairs — creating a beautifully defined, full-brow look that goes far beyond regular tinting. The result is a natural yet perfectly sculpted brow that enhances your features.

The henna stains the skin beneath your brows for up to 2 weeks, and lasts up to 6 weeks on the hair, making it the ideal semi-permanent brow treatment for anyone who wants to reduce their makeup routine. A brow shaping is included in every brow henna service.

  • Skin & hair staining
  • Custom colour blend
  • Brow shaping included
  • 100% natural ingredients
  • Up to 2 weeks on skin
  • Up to 6 weeks on hair
